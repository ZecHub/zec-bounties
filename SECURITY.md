# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do **not** open a public GitHub issue or pull request for security problems.

Use GitHub’s private vulnerability reporting:

1. Go to https://github.com/ZecHub/zec-bounties/security/policy
2. Click **Report a vulnerability**
3. Fill in the details (description, impact, affected code, suggested fix if you have one)

You can also open a private conversation with the maintainers on the ZecHub Discord and mention `@dismad` if you prefer.

We will acknowledge receipt and coordinate next steps. Do not disclose the issue publicly until we have agreed on a timeline.

## Scope

This policy covers:

- `zec-bounties` (this repository)
- The production site at https://bounties.zechub.wiki
- Wallet handling, payout logic, authentication, and authorization in the backend

It does **not** cover:

- Upstream Zcash protocol, Zebra, Zaino, or Zingo issues (report those to their own projects)
- Third-party services (Vercel, GitHub, Discord, etc.)

## What we do not want

- Public issues that describe exploitable problems
- Reports that only affect local development setups with no production impact
- Social engineering or physical attacks

Thank you for helping keep ZEC Bounties and its users safe.
