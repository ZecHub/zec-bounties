# Hunters

Hunter is the individual contributor role. You take work, submit it, and get paid in shielded ZEC.

Onboarding copy: *Take on bounties. Get paid in ZEC.*

## Who this is for

Pick **Hunter** at onboarding if you will apply to bounties yourself. You do not need a team or a shared wallet.

## After you seal as Hunter

1. You land on the main board (`/home`)
2. Add a payout UA in Profile if you skipped it — see [Addresses](addresses.md)
3. Browse open bounties and apply

The detailed apply → assign → submit → payout flow is in [Contributors](contributors.md). Hunter is that workflow.

## What hunters do

- Browse open bounties across communities
- Apply with a short note (timeline, links, skills)
- Submit the deliverable after assignment
- Receive a shielded payout to the UA on your profile

## What hunters do not do

- Create or fund platform bounties as a team (that is [Teams](teams.md))
- Approve other people’s work unless they also created the bounty
- Switch to Team later on a normal account — role select is one-time

## Tips

- Keep your payout UA current before a payment runs
- Prefer a shielded-only UA (Ironwood / Sapling)
- Match the bounty description; vague submissions slow review
