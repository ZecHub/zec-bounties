# Bounty suggestions

Hunters can propose a bounty. Platform admins control who is allowed to suggest.

## Who can suggest

Admins set this under Admin → Settings → Suggestion settings. Three modes:

- **Closed** — no hunter suggestions are accepted. The New Bounty button is disabled.
- **Open** — any onboarded hunter may suggest, still subject to the weekly limit and any per-user block.
- **Threshold** — a hunter must have completed at least X bounties (DONE as assignee) before they can suggest. X is set by admins; the default is 5.

Changes take effect immediately. No restart is required.

## How a suggestion works

If suggestions are enabled for you:

1. An eligible hunter opens New Bounty
2. The suggestion counts toward the hunter’s weekly creation limit and stays assigned to them.
3. Admins can approve, edit while leaving it pending, or decline. Every decision needs a reason of at most 500 characters.
4. Approval activates the normal workflow. The hunter submits work; the team reviews and pays through the usual treasury flow. Declined suggestions are closed and cannot be delivered or paid.
