# Team bounty suggestions

Hunters can submit a bounty suggestion to any verified team from the bounty creation form. Suggestions count toward the hunter's existing weekly creation limit and remain assigned to the hunter who proposed the work.

The target team's owners and admins can review suggestions from the team's bounty program. They can approve a suggestion, edit its title, description, reward, or deadline while leaving it pending, or decline it. Every decision requires a reason of at most 500 characters, which is shown with the bounty in the hunter's view.

Approval activates the existing bounty workflow. The hunter submits work and the team reviews it using the usual submission process; accepted work follows the team's normal payment authorization and treasury flow. Declined suggestions are closed and cannot be delivered or paid.
