# Teams

Team is the organization role. You post bounties, review work, and pay from a shared wallet.

Onboarding copy: *Post bounties. Build with your crew.*

## Who this is for

Pick **Team** at onboarding if you represent a group (project, DAO, company) that will fund work. You can still hold a personal payout UA on your profile.

## After you seal as Team

1. You land on `/teams`
2. Create a team (name, description, Twitter and Discord links are required)
3. Invite members and set roles: **OWNER**, **ADMIN**, or **MEMBER**
4. Optionally attach a team wallet for payouts
5. Upload a logo and banner from the team Settings tab (PNG, JPEG, WEBP, or SVG; logo 5 MB, banner 15 MB)

## Team roles

| Role | Can |
|------|-----|
| OWNER | Full control, including deleting the team and managing the wallet |
| ADMIN | Edit team, members, media, and manage team bounties |
| MEMBER | Participate; cannot change team settings or verify the team |

Platform **Admin** is separate. Team OWNER is not a platform admin.

## Verification

A team is marked verified after **three different platform admins** stamp it. Creating a team does not verify it. Verified teams can be featured more prominently on the board.

## Bounties and payouts

- Team-posted bounties still follow [Creators](creators.md) (propose, review, pay)
- Payouts can use the team wallet when it is funded and reachable
- Assignees are paid to **their** registered UA, not the team address

## What teams do not do automatically

- You do not become a Hunter by joining a team
- Switching to Hunter later is not self-serve on a normal account
- Logo and banner uploads need a configured pinning service in production
